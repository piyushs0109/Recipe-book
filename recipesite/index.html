Kaju Katli


Ingedients


  • Cashew (Kaju) – 1 cup
  • Sugar – ½ cup
  • Water – ¼ cup
  • Ghee – 1 tsp
  • Cardamom powder – ¼ tsp (optional)
  • Silver varq – for decoration (optional)

Recipe


  1. Grind 1 cup cashews into a fine powder.
  2. Add ½ cup sugar and ¼ cup water to a pan and make a sugar syrup.
  3. Add the cashew powder to the syrup and mix well.
  4. Cook on low flame, stirring continuously.
  5. Add 1 tsp ghee and ¼ tsp cardamom powder.
  6. Cook until the mixture becomes a soft dough and leaves the sides of the pan.
  7. Transfer it to a greased surface and let it cool slightly.
  8. Knead gently and roll it into a thin sheet.
  9. Cut into diamond-shaped pieces.
  10. Add silver varq on top if desired and serve.

Ghevar


Ingedients


  • All-purpose flour (Maida) – 1 cup
  • Sugar – ½ cup
  • Water – ¼ cup
  • Ghee – 1 tsp
  • Cardamom powder – ¼ tsp (optional)Almonds & pistachios – for garnish
  • Silver varq – for decoration (optional)

Recipe


  1. Melt the ghee and let it cool slightly.
  2. Mix maida and ghee until smooth.
  3. Add milk and gradually add cold water to make a thin, lump-free batter.
  4. Heat oil or ghee in a deep, wide pan.
  5. Pour the batter slowly into the center of the hot oil.
  6. Continue adding batter until the ghevar reaches the desired thickness.
  7. Fry until golden and crispy.
  8. Carefully remove the ghevar and drain the excess oil.
  9. Prepare sugar syrup and pour it over the ghevar.
  10. Garnish with almonds, pistachios, and silver varq if desired.

Gulab Jamun


Ingedients


  • Milk Powder – 1 cup
  • All-purpose Flour (Maida) – 2 tbsp
  • Milk – 3 to 4 tbsp
  • Ghee – 1 tsp
  • Sugar – 1 cup
  • Water – 1 cup
  • Cardamom Powder – 1/2 tsp
  • Oil or Ghee – for deep frying

Recipe


  1. Mix milk powder, maida, and ghee in a bowl.
  2. Add milk gradually and knead gently to make a soft dough.
  3. Make small, smooth balls from the dough.
  4. Heat oil or ghee on low-medium flame and fry the balls until golden brown.
  5. For the syrup, boil sugar and water together.
  6. Add cardamom powder and cook for a few minutes.
  7. Put the fried gulab jamuns into the warm sugar syrup.
  8. Let them soak for 1–2 hours before serving.

Ghujiya


Ingedients


  • All-purpose Flour (Maida) – 2 cups
  • Ghee – 4 tbsp
  • Khoya/Mawa – 1 cup
  • Powdered Sugar – 1/2 cup
  • Desiccated Coconut – 1/4 cup
  • Chopped Almonds – 2 tbsp
  • Chopped Pistachios – 2 tbsp
  • Cardamom Powder – 1/2 tsp
  • Raisins – 2 tbsp
  • Water – as required
  • Oil or Ghee – for deep frying

Recipe


  1. Mix maida and ghee, then add water gradually to make a soft dough.
  2. Heat khoya in a pan until lightly golden.
  3. Add powdered sugar, coconut, almonds, pistachios, raisins, and cardamom powder.
  4. Mix the filling well and let it cool.
  5. Divide the dough into small balls and roll each into a thin circle.
  6. Place some filling in the center and fold the circle into a half-moon shape.
  7. Seal the edges properly and make a decorative pattern along the edges.
  8. Deep-fry the gujiyas on medium-low heat until golden brown.
  9. Drain the excess oil and let them cool slightly.
  10. Serve the crispy gujiyas and enjoy!
